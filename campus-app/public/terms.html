Who may use the app

Lotus Campus is for students to organize their studies and connect with other students. Give accurate information about yourself and choose your own university and faculty.

University and faculty information is self-reported, not verified. The app checks that the selected university exists and the faculty belongs to it; this does not establish that someone attends that institution.

These terms are a draft for legal review.

Acceptable use

  • Do not harass, threaten or target other students.
  • Do not use the app for scams or spam.
  • Do not create fake accounts or impersonate another person.
  • Upload only files you have the right to share. Do not share another person's private information without their permission.
  • Do not use messages, posts, groups or uploads to distribute harmful content.
  • Avoid pasting private information into the assistant. Text you type or paste can be sent to the configured AI provider.

Reports and hiding

Use the existing report controls for posts, conversations and course uploads. Reports record the reporting account, the item, the selected reason and the time.

Three reports hide a post. Three distinct student reports hide a course upload. Hidden uploads are omitted from the app's list, and the app stops creating new download links for them. Hiding does not delete the underlying record or file.

Conversation reports are recorded; they do not automatically hide the conversation or close an account. Blocking stops new messages in both directions and hides the conversation from the list, without removing its history.

You can delete the text of your own messages, leaving a deleted-message placeholder, and delete your own uploaded files. This draft does not promise a report-review outcome or response time.

Closure requests

There is no in-app account-deletion action. To request account closure or deletion, contact notsnowstorm@proton.me. Logging out does not delete your account or its data.

This draft does not promise immediate deletion, a completion time or deletion from platform backups and provider systems.

Changes to these terms

This draft may be revised following legal review. Check this page for updated text made available in the app. No email-notification or acceptance-tracking feature is described by this draft.
